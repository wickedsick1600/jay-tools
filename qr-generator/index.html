How to use this QR code generator

  1. Enter a URL or text, then choose the pattern, corners, colors, margin, and frame.
  2. Optionally add a local logo. The image stays in your browser and is not uploaded.
  3. Test the preview with your phone, then download a PNG for sharing or an SVG for print.

QR codes do not expire. A URL-based code works while its destination remains available. This tool does not create tracking links or expiring redirects.
